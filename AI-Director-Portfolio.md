# My AI Product Thinking

If I am given a business problem, I would first ask:

**1. What is the actual problem?**  
Not every problem needs AI.

**2. Who is the user?**  
Understand the people affected and their workflow.

**3. Where can AI create measurable value?**  
Automation, prediction, personalization, search, summarization, decision support, or knowledge access.

**4. What data and infrastructure are available?**  
Quality of data often determines the quality of an AI solution.

**5. How will we evaluate it?**  
Accuracy alone is not enough. I would also consider usability, latency, cost, reliability, safety, and business KPIs.

**6. Can we start small?**  
Build an MVP, validate it with users, measure outcomes, and scale what works.
